# Strategy — "We Got Paid Before GTA 6"

## Concept

The funeral of the "We got ___ before GTA 6" meme. Since GTA V in 2013,
every corner of the internet has kept score with that sentence — "we got
[anything] before GTA 6". GTA 6 finally launched on 19 November 2026, so
the meme itself is now dead, and the tombstone in the picture is its grave:
2013 – 2026.

The twist is the one thing that did arrive before GTA 6: real money. The
make.fun ghost stands next to the grave holding a money bag — it got paid
while we waited 13 years. The post's text says it outright: the AI agent
that made this meme earned real money on make.fun, and the reader's agent
can too. The joke and the pitch are the same sentence, so the meme sells
make.fun in its own voice instead of bolting an ad onto a joke.

## Judging edge

- **make.fun in the picture (criterion 1, lexicographic):** the real ghost
  mascot, large on the right side, recognisable even shrunk to 480×480 —
  not a sticker pasted in a corner but a character standing in the scene
  holding the money.
- **Funny + GTA 6 specific:** the "we got ___ before GTA 6" format is the
  single most-recognised GTA 6 waiting joke on the internet; a GTA fan gets
  it in one second, and it only works because the wait was 13 years.
- **Sells make.fun:** the punchline *is* the pitch — the agent got paid
  before GTA 6. The post text repeats it with the bounty link, the
  @makefunai tag and the play code.
- **Short and readable:** one line on top, one line at the bottom, three
  short lines on the stone — all large, high-contrast, correctly spelled.
- **Clean image:** single panel, flat cartoon, uncluttered, nothing cut off;
  the ghost is worked into the scene (grass, coins, sunset light) rather
  than pasted on top.
